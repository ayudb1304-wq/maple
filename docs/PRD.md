# PRD — SkyTonight

## 1. One-liner

"Check one page at lunch, know whether tonight's sky is worth going outside for — and share the verdict."

## 2. Target users

Landscape/astro photographers, aurora chasers, golden-hour Instagrammers, and casual "is the sunset good today?" checkers. They congregate in r/astrophotography, r/photography, r/Nightography, photography Discords. They already screenshot forecasts and share them; we make the screenshot the product.

## 3. Core loop (the reason this retains)

| Stage | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Midday habit: "what's tonight look like?" (v1: organic/bookmarked; Phase 2: push/email at 12:00 local) |
| Action | Open page → location auto-detected or searched → verdict appears instantly, no signup |
| Variable reward | Tonight's score is genuinely uncertain day to day. Clear + new moon + high Kp = jackpot night |
| Investment | Saved locations, streak of checked/shot nights, observation log (Phase 2) |
| Loss (paywall) | Pro protects the accumulated stuff: multi-location, alerts, history. Free never loses *today* |

## 4. The verdict (v1 scoring)

A single 0–10 **Sky Score** for tonight at the location, computed server-side:

- **Cloud cover** (Open-Meteo hourly, evening window sunset±2h): the dominant factor. 0% clouds → full points; >80% → score capped at 2.
- **Moon interference** (SunriseSunset.io moon phase + illumination): new moon boosts astro sub-score; full moon noted as "bright night."
- **Aurora bonus** (NOAA SWPC): if the OVATION grid probability at the user's lat/lon ≥ 10%, show an aurora chance chip; Kp forecast ≥ 5 at high latitude turns the whole card green-themed. For low latitudes (e.g., Bengaluru) this section auto-hides.
- **Golden/blue hour times** (SunriseSunset.io v2): always shown — these are the "when to go outside" numbers even on a mediocre night.

Display as: big score, one-line verdict ("Worth it — clear skies, golden hour 6:02–6:38 PM"), then the detail chips. Exact weighting lives in `lib/score.ts` with unit tests; keep it tweakable via constants.

## 5. The shareable artifact (the reason this spreads)

1200×630 PNG generated by `/api/card`:

- Dark map of the location (Geoapify static, dark style) as the background, subtly dimmed.
- Foreground: city name + date, Sky Score dial, verdict line, golden-hour window, moon icon+%, aurora chip if applicable.
- Bottom strip: app URL (`skytonight.app` placeholder — read from env `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`) + required attribution line in small type.
- The card IS the OG image for `/sky/[city]` pages, so pasting a link anywhere shows the artifact.
- A visible "Share tonight's sky" button copies the city-page link and offers PNG download (uses Web Share API on mobile).

Design bar: it must look good enough that a photographer would post it *unprompted*. Dark, minimal, one accent color (score-dependent: teal for good, amber for mixed, red-grey for bad, aurora-green when Kp high).

## 6. Free vs Pro

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Locations | 1 (current) | Unlimited saved |
| Forecast | Tonight only | 7-day sky outlook |
| Alerts | — | "Only ping me when score ≥ 8" / high-Kp alerts (Phase 2) |
| History / log | — | Observation log + monthly recap card (Phase 2) |
| Cards | 1 style | All styles + no attribution-adjacent app branding removed? **No** — attribution always stays; only the app's own promo line can be toggled |

Pricing (configure in Dodo, not code): $4.99/mo or $39/yr; test $49 lifetime. v1 just links to `DODO_CHECKOUT_URL`.

**Paywall placement rule:** the wall never blocks the first wow. It appears when the user tries to save a *second* location or view beyond tonight — i.e., at the moment they're trying to invest.

## 7. Phases

- **Phase 1 (this build):** everything in CLAUDE.md "What done means". Stateless — localStorage holds the saved location and a simple check-in streak counter.
- **Phase 2:** auth (magic link), DB, Dodo webhooks → real Pro gating, alerts via email, observation log.
- **Phase 3:** monthly "My Skies" recap card, ISS-pass card as a sibling product on shared infra.

## 8. SEO surface (build with v1, it's cheap)

- `/sky/[city-slug]` — programmatic pages for a seeded list of ~50 photogenic cities (file `lib/cities.ts`: slug, name, lat, lon, timezone, country). Server-rendered, revalidated every 6h. Title pattern: "Sunset & Sky Forecast Tonight in {City} — Sky Score, Golden Hour, Aurora".
- Target queries: "sunset forecast {city}", "golden hour time {city}", "aurora forecast {city}", "is tonight good for stargazing".
- Sitemap.xml generated from the city list. Each page's OG image = its live card.

## 9. Success metrics for v1

- ≥30% of visitors generate/see a card (the wow works).
- ≥30% 7-day return rate among people who saved a location (the loop works).
- First 10 Dodo sales within 4 weeks of launch (willingness to pay exists).
If card-shares happen but returns don't → strengthen the daily trigger before building more features.

## 10. Non-goals

Weather app completeness, iOS/Android native, precise astronomical ephemeris beyond what the APIs give, any social feed/accounts-following-accounts features.
